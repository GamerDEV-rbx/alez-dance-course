What You'll Learn

The full
curriculum.

Eight meticulously designed modules, built from foundational movement theory to advanced Alez execution. Every lesson crafted for real transformation.

01
Foundation of Body Awareness
Understand your body as an instrument. Posture, weight distribution, and spatial awareness — the invisible architecture every dance is built upon.
6 Lessons · Beginner
02
Rhythm & Musicality
Stop counting. Start feeling. Master internal tempo, syncopation, and how to let music move through you rather than chasing it.
8 Lessons · Beginner
03
Core Alez Vocabulary
The 12 signature moves that define the Alez. Each broken down frame-by-frame, with mirror and rear-view angles.
12 Lessons · Intermediate
04
Power & Isolation
Explosive movements demand explosive preparation. Learn to isolate muscle groups, increase power, and maintain control at high tempo.
9 Lessons · Intermediate
05
Freestyle Intelligence
Choreography is a cage. Freestyle is freedom. Build the vocabulary and confidence to create in the moment, every time.
10 Lessons · Advanced
06
Performance & Stage Presence
Technical skill means nothing without presence. Command attention, connect with audiences, and own every space you enter.
7 Lessons · Advanced
07
Conditioning & Recovery
The elite dancer trains the body and respects it. Injury prevention, active recovery, and the routines that let you train every single day.
5 Lessons · All Levels
08
The Alez Signature Sequence
The culmination. A 4-minute performance sequence that integrates every element of the curriculum. Yours forever.
4 Lessons · Mastery

Bonus: Live Sessions
